# IESC Vietnam TCL Project — Brand Guide

## Typography

The design system uses two complementary typefaces.

### Bespoke Serif Medium — Display Typeface

Use Bespoke Serif Medium for:
- Cover titles
- Chapter titles
- Section titles
- Major insight headlines

Do not use it for body copy, charts, tables, captions, or sources.

### Aptos — Primary Analytical Typeface

Use Aptos for:
- Body text
- Analytical subheadings
- Chart titles and labels
- Tables
- KPI labels
- Captions
- Sources and footnotes

### Typography Hierarchy

| Element | Typeface | Weight | Suggested size |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cover title | Bespoke Serif Medium | Medium | 26–32 pt |
| Chapter title | Bespoke Serif Medium | Medium | 18–22 pt |
| Section title | Bespoke Serif Medium | Medium | 12–14 pt |
| Insight headline | Bespoke Serif Medium | Medium | 15–18 pt |
| Analytical subheading | Aptos | SemiBold | 9.5–11 pt |
| Body text | Aptos | Regular | 9–10 pt |
| Chart title | Aptos | SemiBold | 9–10 pt |
| Chart labels | Aptos | Regular / SemiBold | 7.5–9 pt |
| KPI | Aptos | SemiBold | 10–16 pt |
| Source / footnote | Aptos | Regular | 7–8 pt |

**Core principle:** Bespoke Serif Medium = editorial identity; Aptos = analytical and data identity.

> Font files are not included in this repository. Install licensed fonts locally. Do not redistribute font files unless the font license explicitly permits it.

## Chart Convention

- Actual data: `#0B2E59`
- Forecast data: `#DDB534`
- Secondary series: `#006A83`
- Gridlines: `#E7EBEE`
- Labels: `#0B2E59`
- Clearly distinguish historical observations from forecasts.
- Use direct labels where practical.
- Always show units and forecast period/vintage.

## Report Page Logic

1. Section/subsection marker
2. Insight-led headline
3. Short context paragraph
4. Hero exhibit or two closely related exhibits
5. Concise analytical interpretation
6. Source and forecast note

## Analytical Writing Principle

Use:

**Fact → calculation → interpretation → investment relevance**

Prefer quantified, source-supported statements. Avoid unsupported causal claims or overly promotional investment language.
